**FAILING QUERY 1: `A[] (amr.EnterAisle imply aisle_clear)`**

This means: whenever the AMR is in location `EnterAisle`, the global boolean `aisle_clear` must be true.

Looking at the model:
- `aisle_clear` starts as `false`
- The `ShelfScanner` sets `aisle_clear = true` when transitioning from `VerifyShelf` to `ShelfVerified` (after x >= 2)
- The `RestockAMR` transitions from `Idle` to `EnterAisle` immediately (urgent location, no guard)

The problem: The AMR can enter the aisle BEFORE the scanner has verified the shelf and set `aisle_clear = true`. Both processes start simultaneously, and since `Idle` in AMR is urgent, the AMR will immediately transition to `EnterAisle` while `aisle_clear` is still `false`.

**Fix for Query 1:** The transition from `Idle` to `EnterAisle` in the AMR must be guarded by `aisle_clear`.

**FAILING QUERY 2: `scanner.ShelfVerified --> amr.Restock`**

The `-->` operator means: if `scanner.ShelfVerified` is ever reached, then `amr.Restock` must eventually be reached afterward.

Looking at the model:
- Scanner reaches `ShelfVerified` and sets `shelf_verified = true`
- AMR goes from `Idle` → `EnterAisle` → `Decision`
- At `Decision`, AMR can either:
  - Go to `Restock` if `shelf_verified` is true, OR
  - Go to `Replan` (no guard, so always available)

The problem: Even if `shelf_verified` is true, the AMR can choose the `Replan` transition instead of `Restock`, and then loop back to `Decision` and repeatedly choose `Replan` again, never reaching `Restock`.

**Fix for Query 2:** The transition from `Decision` to `Replan` should only be taken when `shelf_verified` is false. Add guard `!shelf_verified` to the `Decision` → `Replan` transition.
